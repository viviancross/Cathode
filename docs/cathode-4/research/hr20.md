# DirecTV HR20 / HR21 HD DVR (2006-2010): boot, channel change, banner, sounds

Research notes, 2026-10-17. Scope: the "classic"/blue DirecTV Plus HD DVR UI the HR20-700 shipped with in Aug 2006 and that the HR21/HR22/HR23 (and HR20 itself) ran until the "HD GUI" replaced it in late 2011. Everything below is tagged with its source. **[M]** = official DirecTV user guide text, **[F]** = forum post (DBSTalk / SatelliteGuys / AVS / forums.directv.com), **[B]** = blog/review, **[inferred]** = my inference, not stated by a source.

## 1. Boot sequence (power-on / red-button reset to live TV)

Order, as reported by owners of HR20/HR21/HR22 on the classic firmware. No official document describes the boot screens; every item is forum testimony unless marked [M].

1. **Blue "Hello" screen.** Wording reported as "hello, DirecTV your receiver is starting up" (HR21 owner's list of startup screens, DBSTalk thread "HR21/700 (again) won't allow BIST" https://www.dbstalk.com/threads/hr21-700-again-wont-allow-bist-and-other-oddities.221834/ [F]) and "the blue hello your DirecTV receiver is starting up screen" (DBSTalk, in the 02468 force-download instructions, surfaced via search of https://www.dbstalk.com/threads/hr21-200-issues.192277/ and related threads [F]). A 2011 forums.directv.com HR20-700 owner also describes "the blue start-up screen carried a greeting that began with 'Hello'" (https://forums.directv.com/conversations/directv-equipment/directv-plus-hddvr-hr20700-wont-reset/610c0f7cdf08a4122a467341 [F]). Earl Bonovich's Aug 2006 review calls the same first screen "System Is Starting" and could not capture it (https://www.dbstalk.com/threads/hr20-700-the-review.56780/ post #7 [F]) — so the very first 2006 firmware may have used that wording, with "Hello" appearing later [inferred].
   - This is the screen where pressing **0-2-4-6-8** on the remote forces a software download ("pressing 02468 in sequence at the first picture you see on-screen upon bootup", empegbbs post 9 Jan 2007 https://empegbbs.com/ubbthreads.php/posts/292002 [F]; "As soon as they come back on, press 0-2-4-6-8 on your remote", DBSTalk [F]).
   - Over **HDMI-only** connections "the first few boot screens won't be visible" (empegbbs 2007 [F]).
2. **Blue "Almost there" screen.** Wording reported as "almost there, just a few more seconds please..." (DBSTalk 221834 [F]); "Almost There... A Few More Seconds" (DBSTalk, HR21 reset sequence: "Reset Red Button, Blue Screen: Almost There...A Few More Seconds, DirecTV Screen: Step 1 of 2 Checking Satellite Settings" [F]); "Only a few more seconds..." (SatelliteGuys HR22-100, 20 Mar 2009, https://www.satelliteguys.us/xen/threads/hd-dvr-hr22-100-bootup.168577/ [F]); "Almost There" (Bonovich 2006 [F]). Users refer to it as "the second blue screen" (DBSTalk "HR20 loops and reboots: 'A few more seconds...'" https://www.dbstalk.com/threads/hr20-loops-and-reboots-a-few-more-seconds.158423/ [F]).
   - Pressing SELECT on this screen (later firmware: on the "running receiver self check" screen) opens the hidden BIST diagnostics menu (DBSTalk 221834 [F]).
   - The front-panel reformat trick is done "once the second blue screen goes black" by holding DOWN ARROW + REC on the unit (DBSTalk 158423 [F]).
3. **Black screen, 3-5 minutes.** "After that, you get a black screen for about 3-4 minutes. The front lights flash occasionally" (Bonovich 2006, post #7 [F]). Another owner: blank "for 5 minutes or so before it continued booting" (DBSTalk 158423 [F]). Bonovich warns not to reboot during this.
4. **"DirecTV Plus HD-DVR" main screen → Step 1 of 2.** "The main DirecTV Plus HD-DVR screen follows. This screen will take about 1-2 minutes before you get to the acquiring sat information" (Bonovich 2006 [F]). On 2008-2010 firmware this screen reads **"Step 1 of 2  Checking Satellite Settings... This will take a few minutes."** (SatelliteGuys HR22-100, 2009 [F]; same wording in DBSTalk thread title https://www.dbstalk.com/threads/step-1-of-2-checking-satellite-settings.111857/ and forums.directv.com HR21-100 / HR22-100 / HR24-200 threads [F]). Stuck units sit here ~10 minutes then reboot (forums.directv.com HR21-100 thread https://forums.directv.com/conversations/satellite-equipment/hr21100-stuck-on-step-1-of-2-on-reboot/66ce4572a16f4b1343dddc81 [F]).
5. **Step 2 of 2.** **"Step 2 of 2  Receiving Satellite Info..."** with a percentage; owners report hangs "at 0% on step 2 of 2, Receiving satellite info" and "stuck at 25% on the receiving step" (SatelliteGuys https://www.satelliteguys.us/xen/threads/stuck-at-receiving-satellite-info.273596/ Dec 2011 [F]; DBSTalk HR21-200 thread title "Receiving Satellite Info 0% then Acquiring Guide Data" https://www.dbstalk.com/threads/hr21-200-receiving-satellite-info-0-then-acquiring-guide-data.177517/ [F]). If no signal is found at this point the box shows the **771 "Searching for Satellite Signal"** error (SatelliteGuys 273596 [F]); the manuals print the message as `"Searching for Satellite Signal (x 771)"` [M, HR20-44 guide p.~117; R/HR guide].
6. **"Acquiring Guide Data" / "Acquiring Satellite Info".** After Step 2 the box drops to live video with a message box "Acquiring Guide Data" (forums.directv.com HR20 thread, via search snippet [F]; DBSTalk thread title "DIRECTV HR21 HD/DVR - Acquiring Guide Data...(for ever)" https://www.dbstalk.com/threads/directv-hr21-hd-dvr-acquiring-guide-data-for-ever.112742/ [F]). The manuals: "After these setup procedures are complete, you'll see a message onscreen that says, 'Acquiring Satellite Info', as the Receiver gathers information from the satellite for the onscreen Program Guide" [M, HR20-44 guide Appendix 1; HR22 guide p.86; R/HR guide]. FAQ: "While the 'Acquiring Guide Data' or 'Acquiring Data From Satellite' message is on the screen, progress freezes during download. ... This may be normal. After five minutes, the screen saver will display. Press any key on the remote to see the progress bar again." [M, HR22 guide p.92; HD receiver guide p.58; HR20-44 p.117]. So the message has a **progress bar** and a **screen saver kicks in after 5 minutes of no key presses** — the shape/colour of that bar is not described anywhere I found.
   - Guide data is not kept across a power loss on the HR20: "your guide data will have to rebuild from scratch" (empegbbs, Rob Riccardelli, Aug 2006, https://empegbbs.com/ubbthreads.php/topics/235903 [F]).
7. **Total time.** "The HR20 takes a long time to boot (about 5-10 minutes) ... Doing a software upgrade can add another 10 minutes" (empegbbs Jan 2007 [F]). HR22-100: "it takes 11 minutes just to go through the steps of the bootup process" (SatelliteGuys Mar 2009 [F]). Bonovich's 2006 breakdown sums to roughly 4-6 min before guide acquisition starts [inferred from his per-stage figures]. Software release 0x1FE (Feb 2008) lists "Boot behavior improvements" and "Improved system startup time" (SatelliteGuys https://www.satelliteguys.us/xen/threads/hr21-700-staggered-200-national-software-releases-0x1fe.124605/ [F]).
8. **When you see it.** The full Step 1/Step 2 boot only follows a power loss or red-button reset: "That is the full bootup screen. That should only happen if it has been unplugged from power or you have pressed the red reset button" (forums.directv.com moderator, "Should the DVR always start with the satellite refreshing and installing 1 of 2 steps" https://forums.directv.com/conversations/directv-equipment/should-the-dvr-always-start-with-the-satellite-refreshing-and-installing-1-of-2-steps/610c118bdf08a4122a46dbaf [F]). Normal power-off/on via the remote is standby and goes straight to video [inferred from the above].
9. **Later-era note (not 2006-2010):** the HD-GUI-era boot adds a "Running Receiver Self Check" screen that lasts "up to 20 seconds"; the boot graphic "was created for the early DIRECTV DVRs in 2005 ... It's still just a static picture at standard definition size" (Solid Signal blog, 29 Nov 2022, https://blog.solidsignal.com/tutorials/nice-easy-directv-receivers-always-say-running-receiver-self-check-boot/ [B]). This confirms the classic boot screens were static SD bitmaps, not rendered UI.
10. **Colours.** Every source calls the Hello/Almost-there screens "blue". No source gives the shade, the position of the text or of any progress bar. The classic UI itself is referred to as "the old boring blue interface" (SatelliteGuys 273596 [F]) versus the 2011 HD GUI.
11. **Final transition to video.** Not described by any source. The only hint: after guide acquisition the box is on live TV with the Acquiring Guide Data box over it (item 6) [inferred].

## 2. Channel change

- **Delay.** HR20, Aug 2007 (DBSTalk "Is a 3 second channel changing delay normal w/HR20?" https://www.dbstalk.com/threads/is-a-3-second-channel-changing-delay-normal-w-hr20.88222/ [F]): "there is a delay of approximately 3 seconds every time I change to a different channel" (post #1). Timed by another member: "Native On, switching to channels with different resolutions: 3 seconds. Native Off, or Native on and switching to channels of same resolution: 1.5 seconds" (post #14). Direct HDMI showed the change "about one second sooner" than component (post #17). Explanation given: "everything that you are watching must be stored to the hard drive before you see it". Bonovich 2006: native mode "adds about a half second/second delay while the unit changes resolutions" (review post #8 [F]).
- **Picture goes black.** "the picture goes black for several seconds" (DBSTalk 88222 post #13 [F]). HR20-700 over HDMI with Native on: "tv screen goes black and its says searching for signal and after 6 seconds the next channel appears" — the "searching for signal" is the TV's own no-signal message during resolution resync (DBSTalk https://www.dbstalk.com/threads/native-resolution-issue-hr20-700.88598/ [F]). HR21 Feb 2008: "On a black screen, during the lag time between leaving one channel and picking up the next" picture break-up images "last anywhere from 2-4 secs" (AVS Forum HR20-HR23 master topic p.67, post #1,324, https://www.avsforum.com/threads/directv-hd-dvr-hr20-hr21-hr22-hr23-master-topic.931226/page-67 [F]). Release 0x1FE (Feb 2008) lists "Black screen on channel change" under Improved (SatelliteGuys 0x1FE [F]). Solid Signal attributes modern black-outs to HDCP re-authentication, "3-5 seconds" (https://blog.solidsignal.com/tutorials/screen-go-black-change-directv-4k-channel/ [B]; later era).
- **Banner timing relative to picture.** The banner appears immediately on the key press, before the new picture: "I saw the entered number on the banner, but the channel did not change and the banner disappeared after about three seconds" (DBSTalk 0x12a issues thread, https://www.dbstalk.com/threads/hr20-0x12a-issues.73594/page-3 [F]); "The info bar still works when the screen is black" (DBSTalk 0x134 thread [F]). Release 0x022B (Apr 2008) fixed "Channel Banner updating immediately" (SatelliteGuys https://www.satelliteguys.us/xen/threads/directv-software-release-for-hr20-hr21-receivers-0x022b-4-23.133074/ [F]) — i.e. before that the banner could lag/flip ("the title banner flips back and forth between new and old channel 2x", DBSTalk 0x120 thread [F]).
- **Banner timeout.** Setting is **Menu > Parental, Fav's & Setup > Display > Preferences > "Banner Time"**: "Set the channel banner time to determine how quickly the banner disappears after moving to a new channel" [M, R/HR guide p.73; HR22 guide p.76; HD receiver guide p.41; HR20-44 guide p.85]. Values: forums.directv.com says "Selecting the banner display time offers a range of 2 to 8 seconds" and one user's banner "shortened to 2 seconds after a receiver reset" (https://forums.directv.com/conversations/watching-directv/turn-off-the-pop-up-window-on-channel-guide/610c11cddf08a4122a471b79, via search snippet; page now 404 [F]). A 2012 blog: "switched mine to 4 sec" (https://markwiberg.com/2012/01/08/directvs-new-menu-adjusting-the-menu-scroll-speed/ [B]). Channels-DVR community: an older DirecTV box let you set banner time "to as low as 2 seconds" (https://community.getchannels.com/t/banner-display-time/41694 [F]). **Default not found**; the 0x12a report of "about three seconds" and HR24-era reports of "about 5-6 seconds" suggest the shipped default is in the 3-6 s range [inferred]. The step list (2,4,6,8 or 2-8 by 1) is not confirmed by any source.
- **EXIT** clears the banner early; "there is no way to permanently remove the pop-up window" (forums.directv.com, above [F]). Manual: EXIT "clears things that aren't part of the program (like the channel banner and status bar)" (HR10-250 manual quoted in same search; same behaviour on HR2x [inferred]).
- **Typing digits.** Manual: "If you're entering a channel number that is less than four digits long, the receiver might wait two seconds before tuning to the channel. Press ENTER after entering a channel number to tune immediately." [M, 2006 HR20 guide p.57 (printed p.48); HR20-44 guide p.115; R/HR guide]. 2006 HR20 guide p.11: "simply key in its numbers on your remote. The channel appears after a second or two." and "PREV jumps back to the channel you watched previously" [M]. The typed digits show in the channel banner (DBSTalk 0x12a [F]). **Dash:** "if I type 0 and then any three digit channel number ... a dash appears in the banner after the third digit", and a plain three-digit entry tunes without ENTER (DBSTalk "Dash in the channel number?" https://www.dbstalk.com/threads/dash-in-the-channel-number.57691/, Aug 2006, via search snippet [F]). The remote's DASH key (labelled `--`) is a separate "screen hint" button used for Mini Guide / guide sort, not for sub-channel entry on satellite channels [M, HR20-44 guide pp.13, 23]. Off-air (OTA) channels on the HR20 are entered as major-dash-minor (e.g. 7-1) [inferred from the HR20's ATSC tuner and the dash key; not directly sourced].
- **PREV.** Remote legend: "PREV — Tunes to previous channel viewed" [M, HR20-44 guide back cover; HD receiver guide back cover]. With two recordings running you can "use the PREV(ious) button on the remote to toggle between them" without losing either buffer [M, HR22 guide p.34; R/HR guide p.18]. The HR20 has only one live buffer: "If you change channels, you lose what is in the live buffer" [M, HR22 p.34]. (DoublePlay dual buffers came in Sept 2009 firmware: wkblog https://www.wkblog.com/tivo/2009/09/directv-double-play-dual-live-buffers-on-hr2x-hd-dvrs/ [B].)
- **Audio on change.** HR21 early 2008: "Approximately 50% of the time when changing channels there is a rapid series of clicks (sounds like static)" — a Dolby Digital acquisition bug, not a UI sound (AVS p.67 post #1,335 [F]). "We get the delay in sound coming on" after channel change on HR21, Feb 2008 (SatelliteGuys https://www.satelliteguys.us/xen/threads/losing-sound-when-switching-channels-sd-hd-doesnt-matter.124335/ [F]).
- **Last channels list.** The banner offers "Last 4" ("the last four channels you watched") [M, HR20-44 guide p.27]; HR22-era Live TV Options (YELLOW) has "View Previous Channels" [M, HR22 p.33].
- **Speed vs TiVo.** "The UI and channel changing are extremely quick. It's a great deal quicker than the SD DirecTiVos" (empegbbs Aug 2006 [F]); "at least twice as fast as Tivo in regards to menu loading times" (DVDTalk Sept 2006, https://forum.dvdtalk.com/tv-talk/476029-directv-subscribers-interested-hr20-250-new-hd-dvr.html [F]). Turning Display > Preferences > "Scrolling Effects" OFF "improved the speed when changing channels" (DBSTalk via search; HighDefForum [F]).

## 3. Banner details

The most concrete layout description is the 2006 HR20 manual's "The channel banner" page (ManualsLib viewer p.24, https://www.manualslib.com/manual/40244/Directv-Hd-Dvr.html?page=24) [M]. It describes an overlay "across the top of the screen whenever you tune to a channel" with these labelled parts:

- **Date and time** — "the current date and time". Format not printed in the manual. (Clock can be read any time by pressing INFO: "easily done by pressing INFO at any time", DBSTalk [F].) The "Sat 7:37a" style is **not confirmed** by any source I could reach; treat as unverified.
- **Channel** — "the channel logo, number, and call letters".
- **Program info** — "the current program's title, start and stop times, and rating".
- **Info panel** — opened with INFO: "shows the program description and other details. You can select More Info in the panel for additional options." (So INFO on the classic UI = one-line banner + a description panel; a second INFO/More Info goes to the full info screen [inferred from text].)
- **Current favorites** — "the favorite-channels list in use" is printed on the banner. List names are **"All Channels"**, **"Ch's I Get"** (later guides: "Channels I Get"), **"Custom 1"**, **"Custom 2"** [M, 2006 guide p.42; HR20-44 guide p.27]. The 2008 HR22 guide adds that "A label in the bottom left of the program guide shows which channel list is currently in use" [M, HR22 p.72]. The exact words "Favorites List" appear in the guide option "Change Favorites List" [M, HR22 p.33; HR20-44 p.119].
- **Audio options icon** — "Filled in when alternate audio is available, hollow when it isn't" [M, 2006 p.24].
- **Messages icon** — "Filled in when there are new messages in Caller ID & Messages, hollow when there are none" [M, 2006 p.24]; later guides: "When you have new mail, an envelope icon appears in the Channel Banner" [M, HR20-44 p.26].
- **Parental controls icon** — lock, three states: "locked, unlocked, and temporarily unlocked" [M, 2006 p.24]; icon reference: "Shows that Parental Control limits are currently 'locked'", "limits are temporarily unlocked", "no current limits imposed" [M, 2006 Icon Reference p.104].
- **Button hint** — "Reminds you that pressing the BLUE key opens the Mini Guide" [M, 2006 p.24]. Screen hints are "graphical 'screen hint' icons that display at the bottom left of your screen" for the DASH, ENTER, RED/GREEN/YELLOW/BLUE buttons [M, HR20-44 p.13].
- **Music channels** — banner shows "a channel description and then song title, artist, and record company as each song plays" [M, 2006 p.24].
- **Position of icons** — HR22 guide: "Icons near the upper right indicate more about the show or system, such as a second audio language or a waiting system message. If a program has an alternate audio track, press the GREEN button to hear it" [M, HR22 p.18]. Also "In the top-right of the screen, where the current channel's info appears ... the DirecTV logo" (SatelliteGuys 2013, HD GUI era [F]).
- **Other banner icons** (from the icon references; which ones sit on the banner vs the guide is not stated): "Dolby Digital icon in the program description and Channel Banner" [M, HR22 p.75; HR20-44 p.88]; HD programs show "HD" after the call sign (STZHD, CNNHD) and after the title [M, HR22 p.17]; letterbox icon; "Denotes an error condition has occurred" icon; recording icons: "a program is currently recording", "Record has been set for a future program", series link, autorecord, PPV All Day Ticket [M, 2006 p.104; HR22 p.147]. The record icon and the "R" are **orange**: "the orange record icon appears next to the title", "An orange 'R' icon next to the title indicates it's scheduled" [M, HR20-44 pp.17, 60]; front-panel "RECORD — This button lights orange when a recording is in progress" [M, HR20-44 p.148]. The Keep-until-I-delete marker is "A blue, K icon" [M, HR20-44 p.67].
- **Banner shortcuts (expanded banner).** On the classic UI the INFO banner is a menu: "The Channel Banner also provides easy access to Messages, the last four channels you watched, Favorite Channels, Closed Captioning, Audio and Video options, and Parental Controls. Use the ARROW keys to highlight an option, then press SELECT" [M, HR20-44 p.26]; items are Messages, Info ("Select Info to read a brief description of the program. For detailed program information and other options select More Info"), Last 4, Favorites ("All Channels, Channels I Get, Custom 1 and Custom 2 ... select the Add... or Remove... option to add or remove the current channel from that list"), CC ("DIRECTV Subtitles or Closed Captioning"), Audio/Video ("Change Resolution & Format ... each press of SELECT toggles to the next available option. The current settings are shown in gray text"), Parental ("quickly lock or unlock") [M, HR20-44 pp.26-28]. The non-DVR H2x guide describes "The large channel banner has five selectable icons", one of which starts a recording and "is dim when the Receiver is not recording a program, bright when it is" (H20 manual via search, https://www.manualowl.com/m/DIRECTV/H20/Manual/216781 [M, different model]).
- **Progress bar (DVR status bar)** — "appears at the bottom of the screen"; "For live programs the progress bar is green and the time shown is the time of day"; "If you press RECORD during a live show the progress bar turns orange and the orange record icon appears next to the title"; a white line marks the current position [M, HR22 p.15; HR20-44 p.17]. 2006 guide: stored portion "is orange for recorded programs and green for live TV" [M, 2006 p.22]. Screen saver after five minutes of inactivity on the progress bar [M, 2006 p.50].
- **Colour-key legend at the foot** — no source describes a colour legend on the banner itself; the colour hints (RED/GREEN/YELLOW/BLUE) are the bottom-left screen hints noted above [M, HR20-44 p.13]. Guide: "Color-button hints appear at the bottom of the screen" [M, HR22 p.16].
- **Transparency** — the classic banner has an opaque "blackout area" at the bottom portion that users wanted made transparent ("The bottom portion of the banner should be transparent aside from the part that shows how long the program is and where you are at in the program", DBSTalk "Transparency Of Banner" https://www.dbstalk.com/threads/transparency-of-banner.198564/ [F]). The 2006 banner also had a "cloudy ... softball-sized area" artefact on black (DBSTalk 88222 post #13 [F]).
- **Mini Guide** — BLUE button: "a one-line guide at the bottom of the screen" (Bonovich 2006 post #11 [F]); "special version of the Guide that displays only one channel at a time ... appears over full-screen TV" [M, 2006 p.27]. Guide and menus keep live video "in a small window inside the guide or menu system" (empegbbs [F]; "the HR20 nearly always displays video in the top right corner of the screen", nslog.com 2006, https://nslog.com/2006/12/03/directivo_versus_directv_hr20_hd-dvr [B]).
- **Guide/UI general** — "Guide is well laid-out and graphics are crisp"; "UI apparently identical to the R15" (empegbbs Aug 2006 [F]). HD GUI replaced this in 2011 (DBSTalk FAQ https://www.dbstalk.com/threads/faq-for-directv%E2%80%99s-new-hd-onscreen-guide-release-dates.189524/ [F]).

## 4. Sounds

- **At launch (Aug-Sep 2006) the HR20 made no UI sounds at all.** DBSTalk thread "I miss my 'beeps' and 'boops'" (https://www.dbstalk.com/threads/i-miss-my-beeps-and-boops.62976/, ~Sep 2006 [F]): the OP "really miss those comforting beeps and boops from my old TIVO unit" and asked when they'd be turned on; replies note the HR20 "had a grayed out sound effects tab", and explain the reason: most HD content is Dolby Digital, so "the HR20 would need audio encoding to mix beeps into the 5.1 stream" — "I don't know of any DVR out there, that has audio effects when working with one of the Dolby Digital streams". TiVo "added the sounds over PCM optical output, but they are silent on Dolby Digital". A DirecTV rep reportedly said the beeps "would eventually return".
- **Sound effects were live by early 2008.** HR21 firmware 0x1FE release notes (12 Feb 2008) list fixes for **"No sound effects over optical audio connection"** and **"No bonk over optical"** (SatelliteGuys https://www.satelliteguys.us/xen/threads/hr21-700-staggered-200-national-software-releases-0x1fe.124605/ [F]). So by then the classic UI had (a) key/menu sound effects and (b) a distinct error sound DirecTV itself calls the **"bonk"**. Exactly which release first enabled them (between late 2006 and early 2008) I could not pin down.
- **Setting.** Menu > Parental, Fav's & Setup > Audio > **"Sound Effects: Select the volume of sound effects"** [M, R/HR guide p.72; HR22 guide index "Sound Effects - 75"; HD receiver guide p.43; HR20-44 guide p.88]. On SD DVRs you "press the GREEN and RED buttons on your remote to move between the Language, Sound Effects and Dolby Digital screens" [M, R/HR p.73]. Later path: "Settings & Help > Settings > Audio > Sound Effects > Off" (older menus) or "Settings > Audio > Sound Effects > Off" (Genie) (Solid Signal blog, 20 Apr 2020, https://blog.solidsignal.com/tutorials/new-video-turn-off-sound-effects-on-directv/ [B]). The manual's wording "volume" implies a level list rather than On/Off; the levels (e.g. Off/Low/Medium/High as on TiVo) are **not confirmed** by any DirecTV source I found.
- **Default.** Not stated in any manual. Every how-to is about turning them *off* and calls them "SO ANNOYING", and the 2006 forum tab was "grayed out", so once enabled they were on by default [inferred]. The Talking Guide thread adds "make sure sound effects is NOT set to off" for the talking guide to work (DBSTalk https://www.dbstalk.com/threads/talking-guide.223158/ [F]).
- **Dolby interaction.** Solid Signal: "changing the Dolby Audio setting can change which sound effects you hear" [B]; 2006 forum reasoning above says effects cannot be mixed into a Dolby Digital bitstream, and the 0x1FE fix shows effects were specifically broken over optical — consistent with effects being mixed only into PCM/analog outputs [inferred].
