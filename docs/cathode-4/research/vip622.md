## 1. Boot sequence (power-on to live TV)

### 1a. Normal boot / reset of an installed receiver

1. **Black screen, then the DISH logo "Starting up..." screen.** Full black background; the white "dish NETWORK" wordmark (the 2000s logo with the dish-and-signal mark over the "i") sits in the upper-middle of the screen with a red glowing reflection beneath it; the text **"Starting up..."** in white, slightly italic sans, lower-centre. No progress indicator on this screen. Source: V2 frames at 2:58, 3:06, 3:15, 3:35, 4:00, 4:12, 4:22 (the screen is static for the whole span). The receiver had been unplugged, so this is a cold boot. The 722k stayed on this screen for at least 80 s (2:58 to 4:22) and the next sampled frame (4:30) was black. **[inference]** On a warm reset (front-panel POWER hold / red reset button) the logo screen is shorter; the manuals only say "several minutes" for the whole restart.

2. **Black, then the "Acquiring Signal" dialog** (if the tuners have not yet locked). Captured on a ViP622 in V1:
   - 22:52 to 23:00: dialog on a pure black ground. Rounded, slightly 3-D box with a blue-grey vertical gradient (lighter top, darker bottom, soft light border). Title bar is a darker navy rounded field, text **"Acquiring Signal"** white italic bold, centred. In the box's top-right corner is a small pill with the message number **"535"**. Body text, one line, white: **"This should take no more than 5 minutes to complete..."** Below it a **five-segment progress bar**: five equal grey blocks in a row with thin separators, all empty.
   - 23:10: all five segments are **solid green** (bar is full). Frames at 23:15, 23:35, 23:45, 23:55, 24:10 show the identical full bar — it **fills left-to-right in discrete blocks within roughly ten seconds and then holds at 100 % for over a minute**. It does not loop or restart.
   - 24:30: the dialog is gone and live video (a cartoon) is on screen with no banner visible in the sample; 25:00 the user has opened the guide. So the final transition is a **hard cut from the full-bar dialog to live video**; nothing like a fade.
   - Source for the message-number meaning: ViP922 guide troubleshooting table groups **015 / 018 / 022 / 028** as "You may have just plugged in the receiver and it is acquiring the satellite signal, or the receiver may have temporarily lost the signal" (mydish filestream ID=3143, "Message Numbers", p.4). 535 is the newer (post-2010 firmware) code for the same state; the helpowl thread quoted in search results describes a receiver "stuck on screen 535" with a "0-of-5" bar. **[inference]** The five blocks correspond to the five internal acquisition steps (Bell's version of this screen is literally captioned "step x of 5" in forum reports, "OTA Stops Acquiring Signal on 3 of 5" appears in the EKB L610 release note).

3. **Guide download (only when the guide cache is stale/new).** The 722/622 Set Up Guide's final steps read: "d. Wait while your satellite signal is acquired. e. Wait while your program guide downloads. f. Once your program guide downloads, your screen will display live TV." (mydish filestream id=16204, step 8). The ViP622 guide: resetting "takes a few minutes ... if the receiver must download an updated Program Guide, it may take longer" (ManualsLib 40299 p.120). On the plain reboot in V1 no guide-download dialog appeared between the Acquiring Signal dialog and live video. Exact pop-up wording for the guide step on EchoStar firmware: **Attention 330 "Program guide is being downloaded"** (Bell error-code list). **[inference]** Dish uses the same string; Dish's own manuals never print it.

4. **Live TV.** On the ViP622 in V1 live video is up by 24:30; the guide at 25:00 shows the receiver already populated ("List: All Channels", "2:21pm Mon 7/27", channel rows 9480-9485 with "No Info Available" in some cells because the guide was still filling).

Official description of the reset: "Press and hold the front panel POWER button until the front panel lights blink and release ... The receiver may take several minutes to restart, reacquire the satellite signals, complete any software downloads, and display live video again." (ViP722k guide, ManualsLib 820753 p.140). AVS Forum, 722: "Press and hold the power button in for 5 seconds. The fan will kick on loudly. Let the 722 reboot itself, it will go through the 'acquiring satellite signal' steps." (thread 1093971, as quoted in search summary).

### 1b. First-run / replacement wizard ("Step n of 5", DISH-blue screens)

Seen in V3 (2014 install, ViP-series box) and V1 (ViP622 at 7:00):

- Every wizard page is the DISH blue theme: a medium-blue background, a white "dish" logo and page title top-left ("Download Software", "Connect Receiver to Ethernet or Phone", "Set Video Resolution", "Activate Receiver"), a pale lavender/white content panel with rounded corners, a small step counter top-left of the panel: **"Step [2] of [5]"** with the numbers in orange and dark-blue boxes.
- **Step 1 of 5 — program the remote**: "The following steps should take approximately 30-45 minutes to complete. Locate the receiver remote. While aiming at the receiver front panel, press the SAT mode button on the remote. Press RECORD on the remote. Select 'Continue' when a number appears in the box above." (V3 1:30).
- **Step 2 of 5 — Download Software**: "This step will take 10-25 minutes. a) Getting software from satellite.......... Status: **Installing Switch...** [green progress bar on a light track] b) Updating the receiver.......... Status: (blank) Please do not disturb or unplug the receiver while this message appears on screen. When complete, the receiver will power off and restart before continuing to the next step." (V3 1:52 and 2:00; at 2:00 the bar is ~5 % and status reads "Installing Switch..."; at 1:40 it read "Testing Switch..."). On the older ViP622 firmware the same page is **"Step 3 of 7"**, "This step will take 5-25 minutes", status "Testing Switch..." (V1 7:00). This is where the **Check Switch** happens on first run; on a normal reboot there is no on-screen "Checking switch" step.
- **Step 3 of 5 — Set Video Resolution**: list 480p / 720p (HD) / 1080i (HD), "Test" button (V3 2:20).
- **Step 4 of 5 — Connect Receiver to Ethernet or Phone**: "Testing your connection will take 1-3 minutes. Please do not disturb or unplug the receiver while this message appears on screen. When complete, the receiver will continue to the next step." plus a green bar (V3 2:45, 3:05, 3:20; the bar is continuous, not segmented).
- **Step 5 of 5 — Activate Receiver**: "Please call 1-800-333-3474 to activate", "Receiver ID: R00...", "Smart Card #: S23...", then Live TV (V3 3:48; set-up sheet step 8).
- The set-up sheet then says the receiver acquires signal and downloads the guide before live TV (see 1a-3).

## 2. Channel change

- **Methods**: UP/DOWN arrows (one channel at a time, also on the front panel) or NUMBER PAD; "There are two basic ways to change the channel" (ViP622 guide p.35). Guide: highlight and SELECT (p.36). Browse banner: RIGHT ARROW then up/down then SELECT (p.37).
- **What is seen**: picture and sound drop to **black for about one second**, then the new channel's video appears and the Program Banner is drawn over it. DBSTalk thread 158839: "You would have to get at least a 1 second temporary blank screen because the receiver has to tune to the new channel and catch a new 'key' frame"; one 722 owner found the blank period "far longer than what they remembered from DirecTV"; both 622s showed the same delay; OTA (antenna) channels blanked "about a second". The 8-2009 firmware L622 notes a fix for "rapid channel change black screen" (EKB). **[inference]** The previous picture is cut, not frozen: the box goes to black between channels (the manuals never mention a freeze, and forum complaints are about "blank"/"black", never "frozen").
- **Banner timing**: "The receiver displays the Program Banner for a few seconds at the top of the TV screen every time you change the channel or press the remote control VIEW TV button." (generic DISH DVR guide, ManualsLib 670332 p.68). Neither ViP manual lists a banner-timeout setting; **[inference]** duration is fixed at roughly 3-4 s and is not user-adjustable (no "banner" or "display time" entry in either manual's Preferences chapter).
- **Typed digits**: "While watching live TV, if you know the specific channel you want to watch, enter the channel number using the NUMBER PAD on the remote control" (ViP622 guide p.35). **[inference]** Digits echo in the channel-number field of the banner and the receiver tunes when four digits are entered or after a short pause (Dish channel numbers run to four digits, e.g. 9485 in V1 25:50).
- **Favorites list filtering**: UP/DOWN only step through the active list. Preset lists are **All Chan** ("Displays all DISH channels"), **All Sub** ("Displays all channels you subscribe to") and **All HD**; "If you are on the All Chan or All HD list, channels shown in red or green are not included in your subscription." (Getting Started PDF, PN 181610, sections 5-6). Red = not subscribed, green = can add with SELECT, yellow = digital OTA channel (ViP722k guide p.36).
- **Unsubscribed channel**: message **013/014** — "You may have tried to tune to a program on a channel to which you don't subscribe" (922 guide table); EchoStar's pop-up string: "This is a subscription channel that has not yet been purchased" (Bell list, 013 & 014). Blackout: **011/012** "Program is blacked out" (Bell list; 922 table "Viewers in specific areas are prohibited from watching certain programs").
- **Bad / lost signal on a channel**: the same **Acquiring Signal** dialog as in boot (code 535 on late firmware; 015/002 earlier). EchoStar string for 015/025: **"Please standby for satellite signal"**; for 002: "Signal temporarily unavailable" (Bell list). 922 table for 015: "the receiver may have temporarily lost the signal ... Wait a few minutes to see if the message goes away." SatelliteGuys 179714 (failing 622): "it will display the getting signal screen, sit there a minute and reboot"; "it gave me the acquiring signal message then rebooted". **[inference]** On a bad channel the dialog is the same blue-grey "Acquiring Signal" box with the 5-block bar, drawn over black, with no audio; the manuals call these boxes "ATTENTION" messages but the box title on the ViP firmware I saw reads "Acquiring Signal", not "Attention".
- **Locked channel**: "If you try to access a locked item or open the Locks menu, the receiver displays a message prompting you to enter the password. The receiver allows three attempts ... does not allow you to try again for several minutes." (ViP622 guide p.51). No sound. Password entry uses the number pad.
- **RECALL (PREV)**: "Recall — Go back to the last channel viewed." (Getting Started PDF, remote diagram). The generic DISH guide adds "Press this button repeatedly to switch between the last [few channels]" (ManualsLib 670332, Recall under the Info Button section, p.59). **[inference]** Recall simply re-tunes (same ~1 s black and banner); it is not a two-channel toggle but a short history.
- **Other live-TV keys**: VIEW LIVE TV "cancel almost any menu screen and return to watching TV"; SWAP switches tuners in Single Mode; INFO "view more information about a program or event you have highlighted" (ViP722k guide p.42). SWAP outside PIP did not show the info banner before L448 (12-2007), which added "No info banner when using Swap outside of PIP to switch tuners" as a fixed bug (EKB).

## 3. Banner details

### 3a. Program Banner (top of screen on every channel change / VIEW TV)

Labelled diagram, ViP622 guide p.37 (identical in ViP722k guide p.38):

- A single horizontal rounded rectangle at the top of the picture, dark (charcoal-to-black) translucent ground, white text. **[inference]** on colour: the manual art is greyscale; the live UI of this era is dark grey/black with slight transparency, matching the dark title bars in the Acquiring Signal box; it is not the DISH-blue of the menus.
- **Left cell** (separated by a thin vertical rule): a small TV icon with the receiver number ("1" in a box; the TV2 output shows "2"), below it the **date and time** "**TUE 3/19**" over "**6:25 pm**" (three-letter day in caps, M/D without zero padding, 12-hour time with a space and lowercase am/pm), and below that the mode label "**Dual Mode**" (or Single Mode).
- **Middle**: line 1 program title in bold ("Winnie the Pooh"); line 2 program time range "6:00 pm - 9:30 pm"; line 3 channel in bold large digits followed by the call sign: "**301** HBO2E".
- **Right**: line 1 **rating** ("NR", "TV-14", "PG-13"...); line 2 "**Time Left: 0:45**" (h:mm remaining); line 3 "**List: Family**" — the active Favorites list name ("List: All Sub", "List: All Chan", "List: All HD" by default).
- The Favorites-list toggle was moved "from the date/time banner to the Favorites List name banner" in L443, 8-2007 (EKB) — i.e. the list name is the GUIDE-cycling indicator.
- No "Next:" line in the top banner; the next programme lives in the Browse Banner (below). A "Lower banner popup" (promotional, on Platinum/HBO/Cinemax) was added only in L676, 5-2011 (EKB).
- Icons: an **HD** badge appears beside HD channels in the guide channel column and on the preview tile ("350 STARZ" tile with "HD" bottom-right, Audioholics menu-guide1.jpg); the DVR screen shows a TV-number icon top-right next to the date. **[inference]** The top banner shows the same HD badge for HD channels and a red dot/REC icon while recording; a lock icon is not documented anywhere in the manuals.

### 3b. Browse Banner (RIGHT ARROW while watching)

ViP622 guide p.37: "When the Browse Banner is open, you will see the Program Banner information on the current program at the top of the screen, and on the left side of the Browse Banner at the bottom of the screen. At the bottom right of the screen is information on the program that is coming on next on the same channel."

- Bottom strip, same dark ground. Left cell: browse date "TUE 3/19" over channel "301 / HBO2E". Middle cell (highlighted when selected): current programme "Winnie the Pooh / 6:00 pm - 9:30 pm / NR". Right cell: **next programme** "Flanders the Dog / 9:30 pm - 12:00 am / NR". A right-pointing arrow ">" at the far right "indicates future programs can be viewed". RIGHT/LEFT scroll through time; UP/DOWN change the browsed channel; number pad types a channel; SELECT tunes; VIEW LIVE TV or CANCEL closes (generic guide p.69). Browse banner respects locks: "If you've locked adult channels, the Browse Banner doesn't show them" (p.68).

### 3c. Expanded info (INFO button) and the guide header

- INFO on a highlighted programme opens a full-screen info panel (ViP722k guide p.42; "Press the INFO button to find out [when the movie was made]... Pressing CANCEL returns you to the Browse Banner", generic guide p.69).
- The guide header (Audioholics photo, March 2009) gives the exact text style of that info block: dish logo top-left; "**List: All HD**" (list name on a cyan highlight); "**10:41am Mon 3/09**" (time first, zero-padded day); a "TV GUIDE" logo; a centred description block: "**Unbreakable** / PG-13 Movie. Bruce Willis, Samuel L. Jackson, Robin Wright Penn. (2000) The sole survivor of a horrific train crash... **(HD) (CC) (Stereo)**" then "**Episode: N/A Original Air Date: 01-01-00**"; a live preview window top-right with the channel tile "**350 STARZ**" and an "HD" badge. Grid: date column "Mon 3/09", half-hour columns "10:30am / 11:00am / 11:30am", rows "HD 350 STARZ", "(09:40) Unbreakable" (start time in parentheses when the programme began before the grid), selected cell in teal/cyan, "National Treasure: Boo..." truncation with an ellipsis, right-arrows on cells that run past the grid. Colours: steel-blue gradient header, pale blue-grey rows, white cells, cyan selection, dark-blue "HD" badge. In V1 (2026 firmware on a ViP622) the header reads "List: All Channels" / "2:21pm Mon 7/27", and a 2026 clip shows "List: My Channels"; rows show "No Info Available" where guide data is missing, and the description line shows "NR,TV-14 Movie. Series/Special." style rating prefixes.
- Clock/date formats therefore differ by context: banner "TUE 3/19" + "6:25 pm"; guide "10:41am Mon 3/09"; DVR/On Demand screen "Mon, March 9" with "10:44am" and "9:40am - 11:30am PG-13" (Audioholics menu-DVR.jpg).

### 3d. Pay-Per-View / On Demand event page

ViP622 guide p.43 ("Buying a Pay-Per-View Program"), three-panel diagram:

1. Guide on a PPV channel: rows like "501 PPV — Shrek 2 (All Day, DD)", "Shrek 2 (All Day, DD)" repeated across half-hours.
2. Purchase dialog titled "**Dish-On-Demand**": "Do you wish to purchase this program?" then a field list — "**Event:** Shrek 2 (All Day, DD)", "**Date/Time:** Friday 1/14/05 10:00 am", "**Channel:** 501 PPV", "**Cost:** $3.99" — and three buttons "**Yes**", "**Cancel**", "**No**".
3. "**Confirmation**" dialog: "You have indicated that you wish to purchase a DISH-On-Demand event. Is this correct?" with "Yes / Cancel / No". "Once you confirm an order for a Pay-Per-View program, you cannot cancel the order and you will be billed for it." Purchases list: MENU > System Setup > Purchase History (same page). DISH On Demand (DVR button) menu: "DISH On Demand" title, left list "My Recordings 1 / Movies & More 2 / TV Entertainment 3 / My Media 4 / DishONLINE 5 / Cancel 0" (orange highlight), preview window with "350 STARZ 10:44am" and "Unbreakable 9:40am - 11:30am PG-13" (Audioholics menu-DVR.jpg). Rental prices quoted in the review: free, $0.99, $2.99, $3.99, HD $6.99.

### 3e. Dialog style (for the Attention boxes)

The V2 (722k) frame at 2:30 shows the modern ViP dialog: light panel with a thin blue border, a small triangular alert icon before the first line ("New external device is connected. Do you want to enjoy this?"), two buttons "Yes" (highlighted, dark blue) and "No" (grey). V4 (DISH official) shows the guide-data pop-up **318**: title bar "Error", text "Program information on the hard drive is currently [corrupt]... retrieve program information from the satellite now?" with "Yes / Cancel / No", highlighted button in yellow-orange outline. The message number always sits in a small tab at the top-right of the box.

## 4. Sounds

Short version: **there are none from the box.** Every event on a ViP622/722 is signalled visually.

- Neither the ViP622 guide (154 pp) nor the ViP722k guide (171 pp) has any entry for beep, key tone, button sound, audible feedback or sound effects; the generic DISH DVR guide's index likewise has none, and its only "sound" headings are audio-output troubleshooting (ManualsLib 670332 "Hearing a Program" p.124; 822753 "Using Audio Output" p.117). The ViP922 guide text (2,023 lines) contains "tone" only in the remote **un-link** procedure, which is a 2-way remote feature the ViP line does not have: "select Un-Link on the System Info screen and listen for the confirmation tone" and "Press the STOP button (SAT lights up, then un-link tone is heard)" (filestream ID=3143 p.9-10).
- The ViP remotes (5.x IR "Remote 1", 6.x UHF Pro "Remote 2") have no speaker; the only remote beep in the DISH ecosystem is the Hopper/Joey/Wally "Locate Remote" / pairing beep ("Your remote will beep to confirm that it has paired"), per https://support.dish.com/content/mydish/us/en/support/products/remotes/how-to/remote-locator-on-a-hopper-joey-wally-receiver.html — Hopper era (2012+), not ViP.
- Button presses: no click or beep; the only feedback is the remote's mode LED (SAT lights up) and the on-screen change (Getting Started PDF: "The SAT button lights up"). **[inference]** confirmed by absence across three manuals and forum searches that returned nothing for "beep" on 622/722 threads.
- Error tones: none; errors are the Attention/Error dialogs with message numbers. Locked channel: password prompt only (ViP622 guide p.51). PPV purchase: confirmation dialogs only.
- Audio behaviour that *is* audible on a channel change: programme audio mutes for the ~1 s black interval and returns with the new picture; on an optical/coax Dolby Digital link the external AV receiver may click as it re-locks the bitstream — an AVR artefact, not a box sound (Home Theater Forum thread quoted in search results: "clicking sound was most likely the receiver locking onto the digital bitstream being fed by the satellite box"). Fan noise on a 722 reboot ("The fan will kick on loudly", AVS 1093971) is the only mechanical sound.
- Clean audio samples / isolated-sound videos: none exist to find because there is no UI audio. The "DISH Network VIP 722k" clip https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U8pH0xAct_g (0:00-0:15) is a dead unit with fan noise only. "722K OTA Tuner Audio Glitch" https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TJmqVrNpaZ8 is a tuner audio fault, not a UI sound.
- For an emulation: leave the box silent; if a key sound is wanted for feel, it must be invented (the real one is nothing).
